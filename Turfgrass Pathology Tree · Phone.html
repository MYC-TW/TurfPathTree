How to read this

This is the phone version of a diagram that normally draws the lineage as a graph. Nothing is left out, but nothing is drawn either: instead of lines between boxes, each person's ties are listed on their own record and you tap through them. Someone with a master's from one lab and a doctorate from another appears once, with both ties.

The bands are career stage rather than strict generation, so a few ties run sideways within a band or even upward — two people here each hold a post the other once held.

For two programs, the CDGA and Ohio State, only the succession of posts is documented, so those chains carry no advising ties at all.

What the colors mean
Documented — the tie is stated in a source
Reported — one source, or inferred from strong circumstantial evidence; wants a second confirmation
Unverified — plausible but unsupported
Root — no earlier tie traced

Each record ends with a note on exactly what is and is not established. If you know better than a source does, that note is the thing to correct.
